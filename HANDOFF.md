## Findings
- CastleWorld builds walls, floors, stairs and the moat from scaled cube primitives with flat `HC castle / <id>` colours (no textures, UV 0..1 per face). That is the "flat plaster" in S1/S2/S3, not the Blender props. The props already had metric box-projected UVs: audited in Blender, tile edge is 2.0 m stone, 1.5 black stone, 1.0 oak/cloth/bone, 0.5 metals. No stretch, so no mesh script changes.
- The scene is baked (4 lightmaps, 196 probes) with the dim bake ambient from `HouseLighting.Bake`, so runtime ambient alone cannot lift static geometry. The surface shaders therefore add a global hemispheric fill, and the black lift runs in the camera post-render.
- Black rectangle above the altar (x1207-1352, y470-715 at 2560): I found no asset there. The wall segment is a `stone` cube (x=14, z 4.2-8.8), `Forge altar` has its renderer disabled, there is no sun-window asset in CastleWorld. Likely a lightmap chart/shadow artefact on the wall cube, not confirmed without Unity. Fix applied regardless: `CastleAtmosphere` builds a stone surround plus pointed-arch pane (blackened sun, blue emissive rim and flake leaks, small blue point light) 15 cm proud of the wall at castle-local (13.55, 1.85, 6.5), covering the spot.
- Pale moon: Sky is already 2048 px/face, pale moon angular radius 11 deg = about 22% of frame width at the 72 deg FOV (S4 capture shows ~22%), so no change.

## CastleAtmosphere (Runtime/Castle/CastleAtmosphere.cs)
Add once to any GameObject in the castle scene (e.g. the Game object, only when Map == Castle). Public fields tune everything. Defaults:
- Fog: exponential squared, colour (0.15,0.19,0.28), density 0.038 (S1 camera to far wall 12 m keeps 80% of surface colour, 25 m keeps 41%, 28 m 32%).
- Ambient: trilight sky (0.30,0.38,0.56), equator (0.20,0.24,0.36), ground (0.09,0.09,0.14), intensity 1.
- Shader fill (linear, global `_CastleFillSky/_CastleFillGround`): (0.050,0.068,0.110) / (0.014,0.014,0.024), multiplied by albedo and AO.
- Black lift in `Camera.onPostRender` (additive, linear (0.0040,0.0055,0.0110); pure black renders about sRGB 13/15/22) and vignette strength 0.28. Project is built-in pipeline with no post stack, so no Volume/bloom; the first-person overlay camera (depth-only) is skipped.
- Material swap: `HC castle / stone|wall|darkstone|cobble|wood|darkwood|carpet|cloth|brass|paper|metal|black|earth|blood` become textured world-space surfaces (blood = liquid); FBX props `Castle_*` become the UV-lit variant. Runs at Start, +1 frame, +1.5 s, +7.5 s to catch late-spawned props.
- Reasserts fog/ambient after Game.Awake (which sets Exponential .012 and dim trilight).
