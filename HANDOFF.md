## hc/look5 (round 5): art fidelity, exposure convergence, black standing flames  (WIP list first, values filled in at the end)
Names the layout builder (hc/arch5) can rely on. Everything below is generated by `tools/castle_textures.py` / `tools/castle_architecture.py` and committed under `Assets/HollowCurrent/Resources/Castle/`.

**Stone variants** (world-space triplanar via `CastleArt.Surface(name, SurfaceKind.World, tint)`, or UV via `SurfaceKind.Prop`; 2 m tile, 25 cm courses; textures `Textures/<name>_albedo|normal|mask.png`, Standard `Materials/<name>.mat` for UV props):
- `Castle_StoneWeathered`: weathered grey-blue ashlar (like `Castle_Stone` but different layout/colour variation, heavier chipped edges).
- `Castle_StoneDark`: dark blue-black rough-dressed stone for lower walls, crypt, undercroft.
- `Castle_StoneMossy`: damp stone with green-brown moss and wet patches (base of walls, courtyard, moat edge).
(`Castle_Stone` and `Castle_BlackStone` remain, unchanged.)

**Decals** (RGBA PNG, alpha = coverage; draw as a 1x1 quad ~2 cm off the wall with `CastleArt.Decal(name)`, which returns an alpha-blended lit material, or null-safe fallback):
- `Castle_DecalCracksA`, `Castle_DecalCracksB`: crack networks, about 2 x 2 m.
- `Castle_DecalChips`: chipped/spalled patches, about 1.5 x 1.5 m.
- `Castle_DecalStain`: damp/water stain streaks, about 1.5 x 2 m.
- `Castle_SootBase`: vertical dirt gradient, alpha 1 at the bottom edge fading to 0 at the top; stretch to wall width x 1.2 m, bottom on the floor.
- `Castle_SootRadial`: round soot bloom for around candelabras / sconces, about 1.6 m, centre on the flame.

**New meshes** (`Names` constants added to `CastleArt`: `StringCourse`, `Corbel`, `GargoyleSpout`, `TapestryPanel`; origin = base centre of the back face on the wall, front = +Z at yaw 0, nominal sizes W x H x D):
- `StringCourse`: 4.0 x 0.24 x 0.20 m moulded stone band, runs along X (tile end to end).
- `Corbel`: 0.40 x 0.55 x 0.45 m stepped bracket.
- `GargoyleSpout`: 0.50 x 0.60 x 1.10 m gargoyle head on a spout, projects +Z 1.1 m.
- `TapestryPanel`: 1.40 x 2.80 x 0.06 m hanging oxblood tapestry with border, pole and finials.
