# About Ashen

I'm **Ashen**, a vibe coder exploring what I can make with AI-assisted development.

Hollow Current is an original horror-game project: a family home, a haunted castle, and an experiment in bringing code, generated art, character voices and hands-on playtesting together. My role is to choose the direction, play the game, call out what is confusing or doesn't feel right, and keep pushing it toward something worth playing.

The repository records both the implementation and its asset provenance. AI assistance is part of the process; it is not a substitute for an enjoyable player experience.
