- New materials: 4 of 6 (`Castle exterior glow` on CastleEmissive, `Castle exterior backdrop` on CastleBackdrop, `Castle exterior fog low` / `high` on CastleFogLayer, all created in code; the stone, slate, iron and cloth slots reuse existing materials).
- Renderers: 6 FBX parts + 24 fog quads. Colliders added: 0. Lights added: 0.
- Lightmap-static: everything opaque (keep, gate, towers, walls, rock) goes through the normal prepare path and is marked ContributeGI by `HouseLighting` like the rest of the shell. Backdrop and fog are Transparent-queue, so the existing rule leaves them out of the bake.

## Risks and notes
- Not seen in Unity: shader compile of `CastleBackdrop` (trivial, no includes beyond UnityCG), the colours of the unlit glow and ridge (palette is mirrored in `CastleExterior.cs` and `castle_exterior_proof.py`; tune the constants), whether the fog bands read too strong from the bridge (alpha .30/.21, fade 0.9 to 2.8 m from the camera; the scene fog is exp2 .05, so everything past ~40 m is already fog-coloured except the backdrop, which ignores fog on purpose).
- Lightmap cost: about 4,000 m2 of new opaque surface at the bake's 10 texels per metre. If the bake or the lightmap count balloons, the one-line fix is to treat the `Exterior kit` group as probe-lit in `HouseLighting.Movable`.
- The Count's flights are collider-free; nothing the kit adds is on a perch or a flight line (towers sit outside the courtyard air, the keep stands on the hall roof, the nearest drum tower is 1.3 m from the standing spot with the crenel open). He can still visually pass through the rock apron or a gable if a flight dips outside the walls; none is authored to.
- ImportYaw: all pieces assume Blender (x, y, z) to Unity (x, z, y), like every other kit piece. `AxisCheck` covers a z-mirror.
- Ridge colour is flat per layer plus a base-to-crest gradient and a mist band; it is a card, not terrain: from the bridge looking back (-z) the near ridge is 28 m away.
- The old row of front roof battlements, buttresses and veneer stay; the new parapets stack on them rather than replace them.
