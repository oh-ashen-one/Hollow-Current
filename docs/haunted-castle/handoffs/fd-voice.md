# Piece fd-voice: the Count's voice only from his body (r13 critic section 5)

Branch `hc/fd-voice` from `haunted-castle` `0c2796e`. Model: Sonnet 5.5. Date 2026-10-18. Code, one regenerated clip, one new clip. No engine launched, no build made.

## Owner rule (verbatim, in force)
"Nothing should be talked about as if it's a speaker throughout the entire house." The r13 critic found 8 of 21 Count lines disembodied: `cc_bargain` x7 at the evidence spot (2.1 m from her while he was 15-25 m away) and `cc_listen` x1 two metres behind and 1.5 m above her head; plus the `ear_whisper` watched cue (`castle_hide_whisper`) six times.

## Decisions (per rule)
1. **Every `voice_cc_*` clip plays only through `Presence.CountSpeak`, at `HeadPosition`.** `Soundscape.Speak(id, location, from)` now refuses (drop + `HC_VOICE_DROPPED reason=voice not from the Count's head`) anything whose `from` is not `"head"`; only `Presence.CountSpeak` passes it. `Soundscape.Cue` refuses `voice` and any `voice_*` id in the castle. The old `evp`/`whisper` aliases are gone; Bellwether keeps its ghost-voice cue (non-castle path).
2. **Ear-trumpet (`Investigator.cs`, evidence Voice record):** no voice at the spot. `Soundscape.Evp` plays the new non-verbal `castle_evp_static` at the trumpet in her hand (`HC_EVP from=trumpet verbal=False clip= head_line=`). In addition `Presence.CountBargain()` lets him say `cc_bargain` from his own head, once per run, only if he is in the world within 14 m (`asked: true` waives the off-hunt 180 s gap and the first-8-s hold, never presence/distance/per-hunt caps); logged `HC_COUNT_VOICE ... from=head` and `HC_EVP from=head verbal=True id=cc_bargain`.
3. **`cc_listen`:** `Soundscape.RareLine` now goes through `CountSpeak("cc_listen")` at his head (no more 2 m behind her ear); if he is not in range it retries every 8 s and nothing plays. `CastleScares.PhantomListen` (a lie, nobody there) is now a non-verbal breath: `castle_hide_breath_1` at the same lie position (`HC_LIE kind=phantom_listen from=lie verbal=False clip=`). It keeps its own once-per-run flag and no longer suppresses RareLine (it used to share `ListenPlayed` because both played the same clip).
4. **`ear_whisper` / `castle_hide_whisper`:** measured. The old clip (random vowel formants + consonant bursts, two voices 35 ms apart) has a 3-8 Hz syllabic envelope share of 0.38 and the highest pitch strength of the non-speech set: it reads as a person whispering a phrase. Replaced (same file name and id) with a wordless sibilant exhale (filtered noise, no formants, no pitch; syllabic share 0.03, pitch strength 0.14). Used by the hide beat and the watched cue; the watched cue now logs `verbal=False`. Documented in `AUDIO.md` and `ASSET_LOG.md`.
5. **Sarcophagus gamble (found while sweeping every call site, not in the brief):** `CastleRun.Sarcophagus` also played `cc_ledger_*` from 1.2 m above the sarcophagus. Same rule: `CountSpeak(id, asked: true)` from his head if in range, else the crypt answers with `castle_evp_static` (`HC_COUNT_GAMBLE speech=head|texture`, `HC_EVP from=crypt verbal=False`). Expect it mostly to be the texture; the card text still carries the answer.
6. **Logs and lints:** `HC_COUNT_VOICE` now carries `from=head`; `HC_VOICE_SUMMARY` adds `from_head=N from_other=0 refused_other=K evp_textures=M` (`from_other` counts a voiced clip played from anywhere else and is 0 by construction; `refused_other` counts attempts that were blocked). `tools/check_castle_scares.py` fails if `Speak(` is called outside `Presence.cs` or without `"head"`, if a voice `Cue` exists outside Bellwether's else-branch, if `PhantomListen` calls `Speak` or lacks `from=lie verbal=False`, or if Soundscape loses the refusal/log fields. New `tools/check_voice_logs.py` (log half): fails on any `HC_COUNT_VOICE` with from != head, a non-`verbal=False` trumpet/crypt/lie/ear_whisper line, `HC_RARE_LINE` not from head, or `HC_VOICE_SUMMARY from_other != 0`; `--require-evp` demands a trumpet texture, `--require-summary` demands a summary.

## Risks
- `cc_bargain` and `cc_listen` will now be heard rarely (only with him within 14 m, and `cc_bargain` once per run); that is the intended consequence of the rule. If the owner wants the trumpet answer more often, relax `VoiceMaxDistance` for `asked` lines only (do not move the source).
- `RareLine` retries every 8 s with a cheap gate check (no log spam); it can still never fire in a run where he is never close and quiet.
- The textures are synthetic and unauditioned by ear (no audio output in this session). Measurements are in the section above.
