## 1. Towers
- Walls are vertical: the old plinth flare (r + 0.3) is a 12 cm batter in the bottom 0.6 m only, and the spire cones are straight (no concave "horn", eave 0.12 to 0.2). Corner towers now have string courses, a corbelled crown, a merlon ring (12 merlons) and the cone rising inside the parapet; the gate drums keep their merlons and gain two string courses each. The keep (2.3, 20.4) and the moon clearance are untouched: the ray test still adds 0.0 percent occlusion at all five eyes (`exteriorL_proof_report.json`).
- The skirt look in the critic's frame was mostly the rock apron: it was the same brick stone as the walls, 2 m thick against the tower foot, so the tower seemed to widen into a skirt. It is now a different material (below) with a cliff edge, and it ends in water.

## 2. Moons (`tools/castle_sky.py`, faces `Sky_Front.png`, `Sky_Up.png`, `art/sky_preview.jpg` regenerated)
Ringed craters are gone. The disc is multi-scale albedo (soft broad maria, mottled highlands, fine grain), craters as soft shaded dimples with no rim, a wide soft terminator from a fixed upper-left light with earthshine on the dark limb, limb darkening, the existing glow, and a thin soft halo hugging the limb (pale moon alpha 0.07 and a fainter outer ring, red moon 0.05). Positions, radii (11 and 4.2 deg), stars (point stars from the previous task), aurora, clouds and the gradient are unchanged; the other four faces are byte-identical.

## 3. Rock base (`ExtRock` in `castle_exterior.py`, `Castle_Basalt_*.png`, `tools/castle_exterior_basalt.py`)
- Noise-displaced, strata-terraced heightfield (0.32 m grid, 11.3k tris): a ridged plateau 2 to 4 m high that stops at an irregular cliff 2.4 to 3.8 m from the wall and drops steeply into the moat; eight crag stacks flank the castle. Material `Castle exterior basalt`: seamless 512 px dark basalt (wobbled columnar joints, mottled blue-black grain, strata, damp-smoothness mask) on the same triplanar shader, 0.3 tiles per metre, same raised ambient-fill as the exterior stone. If the textures are missing it falls back to the dark stone instance.
- Rock never rises into a window: `WINDOW_CAPS` limits it below every ground-floor sill (and 3.5 m under the gate-wall banners).
- Bug fixed: the old apron's straddling cells ran through the gate opening (inside the wall thickness) at up to 2 m, so the arch was half-filled with stone from the front. The bridge corridor and the gate opening (|x| < 2.3, z < 1.0) are now kept clear. This may be why the old gate views showed a "closed door".
- Fog contact: the existing moat bands (y -0.42 to +1.05) lie across the cliff foot; nothing new needed.

## 4. Moat (`Shaders/CastleWater.shader`, `CastleExterior.Moat()`, `CastleWorld.Moat()`)
The four moat boxes use `Castle exterior moat water`: dark blood body (linear 0.016, 0.0018, 0.004), five analytic travelling ripple waves, Schlick fresnel (0.03 to 1), and a reflection of the night sky rebuilt analytically from the same constants as the sky script (horizon gradient, both moons at their directions, the aurora ribbon) plus a ray-box reflection of the castle mass (dark), fogged like everything else. No reflection probe, no texture, no extra camera: one pass, about 40 ALU. The crypt blood channels keep Castle_Blood. If the shader is missing the moat falls back to Castle_Blood.
Honest limit: at the S4 eye (0, 2.5, -14, pitch 27, fov 95) the frame's lower edge cuts the front moat (the strip is at -20 deg), so S4 shows a dark sliver of it; the moat reads in the bridge, battlement and gate views. I did not touch the S4 search.
