Interior concept art of the great hall of a vampire lord's gothic castle, wide establishing shot from the entrance doors at eye level. Soaring vaulted ceiling, two rows of stone columns, a long banquet table with the feast rotted to black. Surreal details: candelabras and open books float motionless in the air, pale runes glow faintly on the flagstones in a ring, and the complete skeleton of something enormous and many-ribbed lies draped over the far end of the hall as if it died there. Tall windows show two moons, one pale, one blood-red. Cold blue moonlight through the windows, warm embers in a dead fireplace, long shadows, mist on the floor. Realistic painterly horror style, no people, no creatures, no text, no watermark.
