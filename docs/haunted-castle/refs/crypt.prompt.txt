Interior concept art of a crypt beneath a vampire lord's gothic castle. A central stone sarcophagus with its lid pushed half open, carved with a winged figure. Around it, dozens of wall niches with coffins, some open. A narrow channel of dark red liquid runs through the floor like a river, reflecting faint light, with a small stone bridge over it. Surreal details: roots growing downward from the ceiling that end in tiny pale hands, and runes glowing dim violet on the sarcophagus. Only light sources are a few guttering candles and the glow of the runes. Deep darkness, fog along the floor, oppressive and claustrophobic. Realistic painterly horror style, no people, no creatures, no text, no watermark.
