Character reference sheet of a grotesque demonic vampire, three full-body views side by side on one plain dark grey background: front, side profile, and back. Neutral standing pose, arms slightly away from the body, wings half-folded so the silhouette is readable. Nine feet tall, emaciated, limbs too long with extra joints, spine visible through split skin on the back. Face skinless and distorted: a jaw split vertically into two halves hanging open, rows of needle teeth, no lips, eye sockets that are black holes with faint red embers deep inside, ears torn off. Skin grey-white and translucent with black veins, flayed raw red patches on the chest and forearms with exposed sinew, fresh blood running from the mouth and claws, dried blood crusted on the neck. Hands are elongated claws with six fingers. Wings are membranes of stretched human skin on bone, torn, with hooks, dripping. Only a remnant of a once-noble floor-length black and oxblood coat, shredded and soaked, high collar still standing, tarnished silver buckles, bare clawed feet. Consistent proportions across all three views, even flat studio lighting, no background scenery, no text, no watermark. Realistic painterly horror concept art, body horror, disturbing.
